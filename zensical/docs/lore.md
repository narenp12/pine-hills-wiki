# Lore

League history the scoreboard does not record: disputed trades, curses, and
other incidents. Entries are community-contributed and none are derived from the
captured data.

## Incidents

??? quote "2018 - The Reynolds Reversal"

    **Involved:** [D4rthSi Dragons](owners/naren.md), [Curry's legit team](owners/lokesh.md)

    D4rthSi Dragons lost the [2018 semifinal](seasons/2018-season.md#playoff-bracket)
    to Curry's legit team, 129.76 to 127.56. Jared Goff, started at
    quarterback, threw what was ruled a 34-yard touchdown to Josh Reynolds
    against the Eagles that week; replay reversed the broke-the-plane call,
    marked Reynolds down at the one, and Todd Gurley ran it in on the next
    play. Goff finished with 11.66.

    D4rthSi Dragons missed the final, and Curry's legit team went on to win
    the 2018 championship.

??? quote "2018 - The Tie"

    **Involved:** [Roger That](owners/pranav.md), [Anish's Awesome Team](owners/anish.md)

    In week 8 of 2018, Roger That and Anish's Awesome Team both scored
    143.20. It is the only tie in the captured seasons, across 615 matchups.

    Neither was credited with a win, and neither carried a tie column, so
    both finished an eleven-week regular season with ten decisions: Roger
    That 4-6 and Anish's Awesome Team 3-7. Every other 2018 team finished
    with eleven.

    Both made the six-team playoffs, seeded third and sixth, and met again
    in the [quarterfinal](seasons/2018-season.md#playoff-bracket). Anish's Awesome
    Team won that one 142.10 to 100.70 and reached the final.

??? quote "2019 - Watt Was He Cooking"

    **Involved:** [Super Squirrels](owners/abhinav.md), [Ju Let The Dogs Out](owners/naren.md), [Curry's legit team](owners/lokesh.md), [Roger That](owners/pranav.md), [Sharman's Scorpions](owners/sharman.md)

    With the 35th pick of the 2019 draft, third in the fifth round, Super
    Squirrels selected fullback Derek Watt. The next four picks were Todd
    Gurley to Ju Let The Dogs Out, Julian Edelman to Curry's legit team,
    Stefon Diggs to Roger That and Adam Thielen to Sharman's Scorpions.
    Edelman scored 210.00 in started lineups that season, Diggs 181.60,
    Thielen 108.20.

    Watt never appeared on a Super Squirrels weekly roster. Super Squirrels
    lost the [2019 semifinal](seasons/2019-season.md#playoff-bracket) to Ju Let The
    Dogs Out, 169.10 to 130.40.

??? quote "2020 - Chopped"

    **Involved:** [CHOPSTIX](owners/sahil.md), [My team is Koo(l)](owners/lokesh.md), [Imagine Losing](owners/om.md)

    CHOPSTIX finished the 2020 regular season 2-11, the worst record in the
    league, and entered the [playoffs](seasons/2020-season.md#playoff-bracket) as the
    eighth seed. It had spent the second pick of the 2020 draft on Saquon
    Barkley, who was started twice and scored 15.4. In the quarterfinal it
    beat the top seed, My team is Koo(l), which had finished 9-4, by 118.52
    to 117.52. It lost the semifinal to Imagine Losing, 111.42 to 130.72.

    No other playoff game in the captured seasons was decided by exactly one
    point.

??? quote "2022 - Michael's Marvelous Run"

    **Involved:** [Michael's Marvelous Team](owners/michael.md), [Sharman's Scorpions](owners/sharman.md), [L takes only](owners/lokesh.md), [Jeremy's Neat Team](owners/jeremy.md)

    Michael's Marvelous Team finished the 2022 regular season 5-9 and
    entered the [playoffs](seasons/2022-season.md#playoff-bracket) as the eighth seed.
    It beat the 11-3 top seed, Sharman's Scorpions, 152.22 to 131.46 in the
    quarterfinal, then L takes only 129.76 to 128.90 in the semifinal, a
    margin of 0.86.

    It lost the final to Jeremy's Neat Team, 85.38 to 133.02. Both finalists
    had losing or .500 regular seasons; Jeremy's Neat Team was the sixth
    seed at 7-7.

??? quote "2024 - The Nut Famine"

    **Involved:** [Super Squirrels](owners/abhinav.md), [Kaushal's Potatoes](owners/kaushal.md)

    Super Squirrels finished the 2024 regular season 11-3 and entered the
    [playoffs](seasons/2024-season.md#playoff-bracket) as the second seed. In the
    quarterfinal it scored 61.88 and lost to the seventh seed, Kaushal's
    Potatoes, 116.74.

    61.88 is the lowest score in any playoff game of the captured seasons.

??? quote "2025 - Razor Thin"

    **Involved:** [Save Me](owners/naren.md), [Sharman's Scorpions](owners/sharman.md), [Big black big back](owners/lokesh.md)

    Save Me won its [2025 quarterfinal](seasons/2025-season.md#playoff-bracket) over
    Sharman's Scorpions 106.50 to 105.16, then lost the semifinal to Big
    black big back 123.70 to 124.18.

    The semifinal margin, 0.48, is the smallest in a playoff game of the
    captured seasons. The two games were decided by 1.82 points combined.


## Curses

??? warning "The Bridesmaid Curse"

    **Involved:** [D4rthSi Dragons](owners/naren.md), [Ju Let The Dogs Out](owners/naren.md), [I have Hop(e)](owners/naren.md), [The Herbs](owners/naren.md), [Hill We Go… Again (feat Kyler)](owners/naren.md), [BBigg MACKS](owners/naren.md), [Kamara's a b*tch](owners/naren.md), [Save Me](owners/naren.md)

    Naren holds the best regular-season record in the league, 64-43 over
    eight seasons, has made the playoffs in seven of them, and has not won a
    title.

    Two reached the final: Ju Let The Dogs Out lost the 2019 final to
    Curry's legit team, 123.44 to 156.92, and BBigg MACKS lost the 2023
    final to Super Squirrels, 119.40 to 136.48. D4rthSi Dragons lost the
    2018 semifinal and Save Me the 2025 semifinal, the latter by 0.48.
    Kamara's a b*tch was the 2024 top seed at 11-3 and lost its quarterfinal
    to the eighth seed.

??? warning "The MVP Curse"

    **Involved:** [Sharman's Scorpions](owners/sharman.md), [CHOPSTIX](owners/sahil.md), [Super Squirrels](owners/abhinav.md), [Anish's Awesome Team](owners/anish.md), [L takes only](owners/lokesh.md), [Pukakke NaKupp](owners/anish.md), [Kamara's a b*tch](owners/naren.md), [Roger That](owners/pranav.md)

    In eight captured seasons the Most Valuable Player has never been on the
    roster that won the title.

    Anish has rostered the MVP twice, under two team names, and won
    neither: Kyler Murray's nine swung wins for Anish's Awesome Team in
    2020, the largest haul on record, and Josh Allen for Pukakke NaKupp in
    2023. Super Squirrels held Cooper Kupp for all six of his swung wins in
    2021 and lost to varun's victorious team, then took the 2023 title
    without the MVP.

??? warning "The One Seed Curse"

    **Involved:** [Sharman's Scorpions](owners/sharman.md), [Ju Let The Dogs Out](owners/naren.md), [My team is Koo(l)](owners/lokesh.md), [Super Squirrels](owners/abhinav.md), [Stroud Boys](owners/tanmay.md), [Kamara's a b*tch](owners/naren.md), [Roger That](owners/pranav.md)

    In eight captured seasons the team seeded first for the playoffs has
    never won the title. Six were eliminated before the final - 2018, 2020,
    2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025 - and the two that reached it, Ju Let The Dogs
    Out in 2019 and Super Squirrels in 2021, lost.

    No champion has been seeded better than second. Curry's legit team was
    the second seed in 2019; the other seven champions were seeded fifth,
    sixth or seventh. Five of the eight finished the regular season at .500
    or worse, the lowest being Curry's legit team at 4-7 in 2018 and varun's
    victorious team at 6-8 in 2021.

??? warning "The Sharman Playoff Curse"

    **Involved:** [Sharman's Scorpions](owners/sharman.md)

    Sharman's Scorpions have reached the playoffs in six of eight seasons
    and lost every game they played there, 0-6. Each loss was the
    franchise's first game of that postseason.

    Two of the six came as the top seed. The 2018 Scorpions went 9-2, took a
    first-round bye and lost the [semifinal](seasons/2018-season.md#playoff-bracket)
    98.12 to 148.86 to the sixth seed, which had finished 3-7. The 2022
    Scorpions went 11-3 and lost the
    [quarterfinal](seasons/2022-season.md#playoff-bracket) 131.46 to 152.22 to the
    eighth seed, which had finished 5-9 and went on to the final.

    Two others were decided by under five points: 123.16 to 127.54 in 2021,
    and 105.16 to 106.50 in 2025. The franchise's regular-season record over
    the eight seasons is 59-48.
